African Fashion & Leather House

Founded in Cape Town and rooted in Africa since 2010, Kat van Duinen shapes sculptural pieces from exceptional leathers and natural fabrics. Each piece is designed and made in South Africa by local artisans, and draws on the continent’s craftsmanship, materials, people and art.
